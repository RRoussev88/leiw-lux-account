Learn Luxembourgish, one day at a time.

Lessons and exercises at your own pace, daily streaks to keep you going, and a leaderboard to share with friends.

What you'll find in the app

  • Lessons and exercises

    Videos, multiple choice, drag and drop, open questions, vocabulary and grammar: every lesson can be practised in several ways.

  • Learn offline

    Download lessons to study without a connection; your progress syncs afterwards.

  • Daily streaks

    Keep going day after day. Freeze tokens protect your streak if you miss a day.

  • Friends and leaderboard

    Follow friends and compare your progress on the leaderboard.

  • Four languages

    The app is available in French, English, German and Luxembourgish.

Who is it for?

For anyone who lives, works or studies in Luxembourg, and anyone who simply likes the language.
